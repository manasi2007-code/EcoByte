What Is Inside Your Phone?

A typical modern smartphone contains over 60 different elements from the periodic table, including precious metals, rare earth elements, and hazardous chemical toxins. Click any node on the schematic or buttons below.

Did you know? 1 metric ton of discarded smartphones yields approximately 300 grams of pure Gold, compared to only 5 grams from 1 ton of primary gold ore!
DO

Recommended Practices

  • Factory Reset and Wipe Personal Data: Delete all Google/Apple accounts and format drives before giving away devices.
  • Tape Battery Terminals: Cover contacts of loose lithium-ion batteries with non-conductive electrical tape to prevent short-circuit fires.
  • Keep Original Accessories Together: Bundling cables, chargers, and keyboards improves recovery yield and material segregation.
  • Use Certified WEEE/CPCB Recyclers: Ensure the vendor provides traceable manifests and certified zero-landfill documentation.
  • Store In Dry, Cool Environment: Keep old electronics away from rainwater and moisture to stop electrolyte leakage.
DONT

Prohibited Actions

  • Never Mix With Kitchen/Municipal Waste: Compacted garbage trucks can puncture lithium cells, triggering aggressive explosions.
  • Never Burn Cables or Wires: Open combustion releases deadly dioxins, furans, and carcinogenic black carbon into local breathing air.
  • Do Not Hand Over To Informal Ragpickers: Acid-bath extraction in backyards poisons rivers, soil, and causes severe neurological harm to workers.
  • Do Not Smash CRT Screens or Tubes: Older cathode ray tubes contain up to 2-3 kilograms of poisonous lead dust under vacuum pressure.
  • Avoid Storing Dead Batteries In Hot Drawers: Swollen or bloated lithium polymer pouches can spontaneously ignite when crushed.
